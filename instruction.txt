FIRST-TIME SETUP (3 Simple Steps)
============================================================

When you login for the first time, you'll see the Setup Wizard:

STEP 1: Select Your Country
-----------------------------
Click on your country:
   Asia:     Nepal, India, Singapore, Japan, UAE
   Europe:   UK, Germany, France, Ireland
   Americas: USA, Canada
   Oceania:  Australia, New Zealand
   Africa:   South Africa

STEP 2: Enter Company Details
-------------------------------
- Company Name: Your business name
- Address: Business address
- Phone: Contact number

STEP 3: Choose Date Format
----------------------------
- BS (Bikram Sambat): Nepal only
- AD: All other countries

Click "Complete Setup" → You're ready!

============================================================
COMMON TASKS
============================================================

ADD A NEW PRODUCT:
1. Click "Products" in sidebar
2. Click "Add Product"
3. Enter name, price, stock, category
4. Click Save

RECORD A SALE:
1. Click "POS" in sidebar
2. Search products
3. Add to cart
4. Select payment method
5. Click "Complete Sale"

VIEW TAX REPORT:
1. Click "[Your Tax] Report" in sidebar
2. Select date range
3. View output tax (collected) and input tax (paid)
4. See net payable amount

ADD A STAFF MEMBER:
1. Click "User Management" in sidebar
2. Click "Add User"
3. Enter name, email, password
4. Assign role and permissions
5. Click Save
